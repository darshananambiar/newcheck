The Story of Sashka Matka

SASHKA MATKA started with a passion to create a tea experience people could emotionally connect with.

Tea has always been part of everyday life — conversations, friendships, family time, work breaks, celebrations, and peaceful evenings. But most tea spaces felt repetitive and ordinary.

We wanted to change that.

The idea behind SASHKA MATKA was simple: Create a modern tea destination where flavours, atmosphere, comfort, presentation, and experiences come together beautifully.
